Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings, not within MindBubble directly. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: open the Play Store app → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions.

I redeemed a gift or promo code and it's not showing up.
Try closing and reopening the app. If it still doesn't appear after a minute, email us at the address above with the account email you used and we'll look into it.

The widget isn't updating.
Open the MindBubble app at least once — the widget reads its content from a cache the app keeps stocked, so it needs the app to have been opened at some point to have anything to show.

How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Delete Account - see our account deletion instructions for details.
