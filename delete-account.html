Delete Your MindBubble Account

You can permanently delete your MindBubble account and its data directly from within the app - no need to contact us.

Steps to delete your account

  1. Open the MindBubble app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon on the daily affirmation screen).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account is deleted immediately once confirmed - this can't be undone.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, active pack selections, and purchase-history record from our systems, along with your login credentials.

What isn't automatically cancelled

Deleting your MindBubble account does not cancel an active subscription - subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple or Google, not by us. To stop being charged, cancel separately:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions
  • Android: Play Store app → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions
