# MindBubble Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MindBubble ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the MindBubble mobile application and home screen widget (the "App"). By using MindBubble, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

**Account Information**
When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).

**Purchase Information**
When you subscribe or purchase a content pack, payment is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, and purchase validation is handled by RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details — these are held by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their own privacy policies.

**Usage Data**
We collect data about which content packs you own, your selected daily content preferences (e.g. active pack, rotation settings), and basic app usage/crash information to help us maintain and improve the App.

**Device Information**
We may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system version, and app version, primarily for crash reporting and compatibility purposes.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide and maintain your account and daily widget content
- Process and validate purchases and subscriptions
- Diagnose and fix technical issues (via crash reporting)
- Communicate with you about your account or purchases, when necessary
- Improve the App's features and content over time

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 4. Third-Party Services

MindBubble relies on the following third-party services to operate:

- **Supabase** — hosts our database, including account and content data
- **RevenueCat** — manages and validates subscription and purchase entitlements
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — process all payments and subscriptions
- **Sentry** — provides crash and error reporting to help us fix bugs

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on our behalf.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices, including encrypted connections and access controls (Row Level Security) that restrict who can read or modify your information. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Children's Privacy

MindBubble is not directed at, or intended for use by, children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You can also delete your account directly within the App's settings, where available.

## 8. International Users

MindBubble is available internationally. By using the App, you understand that your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including countries whose data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by other reasonable means. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
